# **2. Placing the components for the pcb**

## **Placing the components to handle footprints and fixing the flipped issue - 8/2/2026 (4 Hours & 30 Minutes)
After finishing the schematic, I worked on placing the components to get a general scope on where I would place the Raspberry pi pico to make the pcb be as clean and compact as it can.
I placed all the hotswap sockets but didn't realize they actually went on the back of the keyboard, so I checked the part I was going to use (Kailh hotswap socket) and verified, but it made the keyboard layout be mirrored left to right. In the end, I decided that the amount of pins available wasn't going to be enough and that the schematic had to be redone for the new matrix I needed and the different parts I wanted to use.
