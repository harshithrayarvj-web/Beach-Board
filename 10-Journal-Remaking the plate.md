# *Replacing the generated plate with one that was made with the svg of the front courtyard layer (8/10/2026)*- (1 Hour & 25 Minutes)
I changed the generated plate that I used before with one that was an extrusion of a sketch I made with the svg of the front courtyard layer on my pcb, and extra lines to create the walls.
I did this because I wasn't sure if generated plates were allowed, so I decided not to risk it and also took the chance to add larger holes for the stabilizers to be on the safer side.
I also made the walls thicker in general on the plate, but made them especially thick near the bottom row since it would face the most stress from my palms.
