# *Finishing the case design*

## *Adding walls to the plate and designs to the screen cover (8/8/2026)* - (5 Hours)
I decided to go back to the screen cover and change it's design to not make the keyboard's side feel empty, and I made it slope down off the side and also add designs to make it more beach related.
The screen cover has rounded rectangles to make it look vaporware and to extend the screen opening and fill the space below it, and the side has wave like designs to help fill that large area
while not popping out too much to be uncomfortable. I also added walls to the plate instead of adding something to clamp down on the plate like on a sandwich mount or gasket mount. I made spots for adding heatset inserts
however I need to make sure the design works tomorrow with the screws I plan on buying. In the end I decided to order most of the parts I need for the keyboard from digikey because they readily have the mcp23017
and micro sd socket I need, but they had a shipping fee so I ended up getting all the parts I need from there to make up for the shipping fee (but they also have a tarrif cost of around 3 dollars for my diodes),
outside of the screen and rotary encoder, which I'll get from aliexpress.
