# **Placing the new components on the PCB and routing them**

## **Placing the components and routing them to the pi pico - 8/2/2026 (6 Hours & 15 Minutes)
After I modified the schematic and rearanged the parts, I started on routing all the connections.
Here I had the issue of not being able to cleanly route wires due to my matrix layout and the mcp component, which ended up making me use a lot of vias.
I also reduced the size of the board after routing the pins on the pico that were close to the usb port so a usb cable could easily connect to the raspberry pi pico.
Later I would have to route the capacitors and the pull up resistors (after I change from a SMD footprint resistor) for the mcp and the microsd socket. 
Majority of the time spent was on doublechecking the parts I could get and moving parts around on the pcb to make things cleaner.
