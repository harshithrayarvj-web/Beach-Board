# *Adding the Silkscreen*

## *Adding the silkscreen* (8/4/2026 - 8/5/2026) (10 Hours)
Now I moved on to designing the silkscreen, and by far this took me the longest out of all the steps, with around 10 hours.
It sure took me a long time, but I ended up going with the initial test design that I used to check how it looked in white and black on jlcpcb.
I made 2 different designs but they weren't cohesive with the cover art for the TOH comic book, and I've still got a long way to go with art, so I just decided to keep it minimalist and clean.
