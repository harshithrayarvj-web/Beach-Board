## **Placing the components to handle footprints and fixing the flipped issue
After finishing the schematic, I worked on placing the components to get a general scope on where I would place the Raspberry pi pico to make the pcb be as clean and compact as it can.
I placed all the hotswap sockets but didn't realize they actually went on the back of the keyboard, so I checked the part I was going to use (Kailh hotswap socket) and verified, but it made the keyboard layout be mirrored left to right.
